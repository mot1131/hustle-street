House rules

Quick takes about 20 minutes, Standard 35, Long 50. With the jackpot on, tax, fines and bills go into a pot that whoever lands on Gap Year wins. If the timer runs out, the game plays a safe move for you.

    How to play
    • Joining: one person taps Host and shares the room code. Everyone else opens the link and joins. Keep the host's screen on. If the host closes the page the game pauses until they reopen it and tap Resume.
    • Moving: spin 1–10 and go clockwise. First turn, choose uni (costs $40k, better-paid jobs) or start working (a $40k head start, lower pay).
    • Payday: each time you pass it you collect your salary, minus $3k per kid. Negative cash costs 10% interest (max $15k a Payday). Rent can't push you more than $100k into the red: the landlord just gets what you can scrape together. If you're the furthest behind by $80k or more, the mates chip in an extra $15k.
    • Suburbs: buy them when you land on an empty one. Others pay you rent. Own both suburbs of a colour and rent doubles.
    • Auctions: if you pass on a suburb (or can't afford it), everyone takes turns bidding. You can only buy a suburb outright if you'd still have $30k left for rent. Bidding opens at half the list price, so nobody gets a suburb for a steal. Fold to drop out; the last bidder standing wins.
    • Houses and hotels: on your turn, before you spin, build on any suburb where you own the whole colour. 1 house triples the rent, 2 houses is ×4, a hotel is ×6. Each costs half the suburb's price. Reno spaces let you add one anywhere you own, set or not.
    • Trading: on your turn, before you spin, offer suburbs and cash to another player.
    • Bank of Mum and Dad: borrow money on your turn (the amount is a house rule). You lose 2 joy each Payday until you pay it back; repaying earns 3 joy. Anything owed comes off your final score.
    • Joy comes from weddings, babies, holidays, gap years and good Life cards. Each point is worth $10k at the end. You also get $35k per kid and $40k for being married.
    • Life cards include a few sneaky ones: Career Swap and Robin Hood let you pick a target.
    • Lost your phone? Open the invite link on any device and tap your name to rejoin.
    • After your last lap you retire (first retiree gets $30k, second $15k, third $5k). Retired players still collect rent. When everyone has retired, the highest net worth wins.
