    How to play
    • Spin 1–10 and move clockwise. First turn, choose: pay $40k for uni (better-paid jobs) or start working straight away.
    • Payday: every time you pass it you collect your salary, minus $3k per kid. Negative cash costs 10% interest.
    • Playing: one person taps Host and shares the room code. Everyone else opens the link and joins. Keep the host’s screen on: if the host closes the page the game pauses until they reopen it (it’s saved on their phone).
    • Suburbs: buy them when you land on an empty one. Others pay you rent. Own both suburbs of a colour and rent doubles. Reno tiles let you renovate one of yours: rent doubles again and it's worth 1.5× at the end.
    • Life tiles draw a random event. Wedding, Baby, Holiday and Gap Year earn joy points. Job Fair lets you switch careers; Uni gets you a degree.
    • Bank of Mum and Dad: on your turn, borrow $50k. Until you pay it back you lose 2 joy every Payday; paying it back earns 3 joy. Anything still owed comes off your final score.
    • Joy points come from weddings, babies, holidays, gap years and good life cards. Each one is worth $5k at the end.
    • After your final lap you retire (first retiree gets $30k, second $15k, third $5k). Retired players still collect rent.
    • When everyone's retired: cash + property value + $5k per joy point. Highest net worth wins.
